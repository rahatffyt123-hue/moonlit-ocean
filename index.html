✧   A NOTE FOR YOUR HEART   ✧

Dear Moon,

There is a quiet kind of beauty in you that does not need to ask the world for permission to shine.

Your eyes feel like the sea just before sunrise—deep, thoughtful, and full of stories words could never completely hold. They do not have to hide every cloud to remain beautiful.

Your voice, I imagine, is the kind of gentle melody that makes a difficult day feel a little less heavy. Even when you speak softly, your words deserve to be heard.

And your personality? It is the rare warmth of a small light kept alive through a long winter: thoughtful, quietly lovely, and meaningful in ways you may not always notice in yourself.

If today has been unkind, please do not mistake a painful moment for the whole story of your life. You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to feel. You do not have to be strong every second to be worthy of tenderness.

The ocean never apologizes for having waves.
Neither should you, Moon.

With gentleness, for MOON ♡
